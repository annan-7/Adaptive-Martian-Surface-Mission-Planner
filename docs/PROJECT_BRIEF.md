# MARS COMPASS v0.3

A science-first, map-centered educational prototype for NASA Space Apps' Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map concept. Astronaut and Earth Planner perspectives explore Jezero science, delayed evidence and explicitly conditional recommendations.

The unproven research hypothesis is that linking changed observations to scientific objectives and recommendation assumptions improves understanding compared with a layered map (A) or the same map plus a timeline (B). Condition C adds these links without adding facts. This prototype supports comparison; it does not establish superiority.

S1 concerns ancient river/delta deposition into a lake, grounded in Kodiak's reported stratigraphy. S2 concerns later energetic flooding, grounded in reported boulder-bearing scarp deposits. Neither is an asserted astronaut sampling site. No claim is made that two real outcrops are reachable in one EVA.

The real regional science map is separate from fictional route geometry. No flight planning, operational safety certification, live NASA feed, autonomous mission control or AI inference is provided.
